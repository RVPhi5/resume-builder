ROHAN VITTAL — APPLICATION GAP LEDGER
================================================================================

One entry per tailored resume. A "gap" is something the job description asked
for that master.txt cannot honestly support — a named technology, a credential,
a domain, or an eligibility condition. It is NOT something that was merely cut
for space: a bullet dropped to fit one page is a selection decision and belongs
in the build's summary, not here.

The point of the file is the pattern across applications. The same gap showing
up in five postings is a thing worth actually going and learning, which no
single build summary would ever tell you.

Conventions
  - Append new entries at the BOTTOM, oldest first, so the file reads as a
    history. Never rewrite an older entry to reflect skills acquired later.
  - Each entry: Company, Title, then the JD and resume it came from, then the
    gaps, then a horizontal rule.
  - Mark each gap HARD (the JD names it and master.txt has nothing at all) or
    SOFT (partial or adjacent backing exists, but the claim is a stretch).
    HARD gaps are the ones that accumulate into something actionable.
  - State the nearest real thing in master.txt, where there is one. That is
    what makes a gap answerable in an interview rather than just absent.
  - Entries marked [RECONSTRUCTED] were written after the fact from the JD and
    the compiled resume, not during that build.
