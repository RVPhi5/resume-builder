% Section headings are the document's structure, and a parser reads structure
% out of the text layer, not off the page. \scshape renders PROJECTS but
% *extracts* "Projects" -- small capitals sit in CMCSC10's a-z slots -- so the
% heading arrives lowercase and unbolded while \textbf employer names arrive
% bold. A parser then ranks the employer above the section and folds the whole
% Projects block into the last job. \MakeUppercase puts real capitals in the
% text layer and \bfseries sets the bold flag. The glyph remap below cannot fix
% this: CMCSC10's small caps share glyph names with ordinary lowercase, so
% remapping them would corrupt every body word.
%
% The other half is vertical. A heading has to sit nearer the section it opens
% than the one it ends, or whitespace segmentation attaches it upward and the
% section's whole contents land inside the last entry above it. Only the space
% *before* the heading controls this: the \vspace after \titlerule is glue at
% the top of a list and LaTeX discards it, which is why -5pt and -11pt there
% render identically. So the binding is tuned by the leading \vspace alone --
% -3pt rather than -4pt, since full capitals also stand taller than small caps
% and push the rule down on their own. check.py enforces both halves.
\titleformat{\section}{
  \vspace{-3pt}\bfseries\raggedright\large
}{}{0em}{\MakeUppercase}[\color{black}\titlerule \vspace{-5pt}]
